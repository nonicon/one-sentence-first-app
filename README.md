> Just tell make.fun the app idea you've been carrying around in your head, and by tonight you'll be tapping through a real, working version of it — no code, no cost, just your idea finally alive on your screen.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (created if missing). The gate requires UTF-8 text of 1 to 240 characters; this entry is 209.
